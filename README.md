## 📝 About the Game
You play as an employee at Mensa Brand Technologies. Your ultimate goal? Secure permanent Work From Home (WFH) by proving you can successfully waste time in the office without management noticing. Accumulate **100 Slack Points** to win, but beware: getting caught by the patrolling managers 3 times means you're fired!

## ✨ Features
* **4 Unique Personas:** Play as Avinash (Speed), Afroz (Tech), Sneha (Networking), or Ritu (Stealth), each with unique passives.
* **Dynamic AI & Stealth:** Managers have flashlight vision cones. Hide behind server racks or use your wandering coworkers as moving crowd-cover!
* **Quick Time Events (QTE):** High-risk slacking tasks like Ping-Pong or making Coffee require quick reflexes.
* **Combo System:** Chain slacking events across different departments to rack up huge multiplier bonuses.
* **Cross-Platform:** Fully playable on both Desktop (Keyboard) and Mobile (Touch Joystick).

## 🕹️ Controls
**Desktop:**
* **W, A, S, D** or **Arrow Keys**: Move
* **Spacebar** or **E**: Interact / Resolve QTE

**Mobile:**
* **Virtual Joystick** on the left to move.
* **Interact Button** on the right to perform actions.

## 🏢 How to Play
1. **Explore the Office:** Walk to highlighted zones (Kitchen, Bathrooms, Empty Desks, TT Table).
2. **Slack Off:** Hit interact to start slacking. Keep an eye out for patrolling managers (Prem, Sandhya, Ramya).
3. **Manage Suspicion:** If a manager spots you, your suspicion meter fills up. If it hits 100%, you get a strike.
4. **Use Distractions:** Grab Espresso for a speed boost, or ring empty desk phones to lure managers away from their paths.
